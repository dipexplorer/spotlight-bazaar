## 2. High-Conversion Bio Blueprint (Zero-Slop Copy)

Avoid generic AI slop (e.g. *"Empowering entrepreneurs 🚀 DM for collabs 👇"*).  
Instead, use the exact **movement-based psychological formula** that made *India Again* go viral:

```text
Building India’s open business street, with you. 🇮🇳
Comment your business & city to claim your storefront.
Day 1 · Live Directory 📍
⚡ Engineered by @nq_insta
🔗 spotlight.nerqiva.com
```

### Why this Bio Works:
1. **Line 1 (The Movement):** *"Building India's open business street, with you."* — Framed as a collective national project, not an agency sales pitch.
2. **Line 2 (The Direct Call-To-Action):** *"Comment your business & city..."* — Tells profile visitors the exact action to take.
3. **Line 3 (The Social Proof / Live Stat):** *"Day 1 · Live Directory 📍"* — Creates day-by-day narrative tension (just like Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 of India Again).
4. **Line 4 (The Authority Anchor):** *"⚡ Engineered by @nq_insta"* — Channels trust and engineering credibility directly back to Nerqiva.
